Making this legally official

Printing a document doesn't make it BIR-legal on its own — only your BIR Regional District Office (RDO) can actually approve/stamp these. This POS gets the paperwork ready; these steps are still yours to do in person or via your RDO/accountant:

  1. File BIR Form 1900 (Permit to Use Loose-Leaf Books of Accounts) at your RDO. Once approved, print the ledgers above at the end of each period, bind them, and bring them back for stamping by the deadline (generally within 15 days after the taxable year, then annually on renewal).
  2. Ask your RDO whether this POS also needs CAS (Computerized Accounting System) accreditation so its Sales Invoices count as officially system-generated — this matters more once you're issuing higher volumes or have staff issuing invoices, which is your case.
  3. Once you receive either number, enter it under "ATP / CAS Acknowledgement Certificate no." in Business & BIR info above — it'll then print on every invoice automatically.

None of this can be done from software alone — it needs an actual visit or filing with BIR.

Printer, scanner & drawer

Barcode scanning

Tap the 📷 button next to search on the Sell tab to scan with your camera — works on any phone, no setup needed.

Or pair a Bluetooth barcode scanner in your phone or computer's Bluetooth settings — most scanners connect like a wireless keyboard, so no app is needed. Once paired, just scan while this Sell tab is open and it'll add the matching product to your cart automatically. Make sure each product has its barcode saved (edit the product → Barcode field).

Bluetooth receipt printer

The Print button on a completed sale uses your device's normal print option — this works with most receipt printers that came with an Android or iPhone printing app, and is the most reliable choice.

Some printers also support connecting directly from this browser (Chrome, on Android or a computer only — not Safari or iPhone). Not every printer model supports this, but you can try it below — if it doesn't work, the Print button above will still work.

So connect your printer above first, then use the button below to open the drawer. It'll also open automatically every time you complete a sale.
